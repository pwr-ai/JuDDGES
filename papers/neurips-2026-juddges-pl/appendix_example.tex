wskazał Pan, że w dniu 9 lutego 2016 r. dokonał Pan zajęcia nieruchomości należącej do dłużnika. Nieruchomość została nabyta przez dłużnika 4 września 2000 r. i stanowiła grunt zabudowany pałacem o pow. użytkowej 997,2 m\textsuperscript{2} oraz dwoma budynkami gospodarczymi. you indicated that on 9 February 2016 you carried out the seizure of a property belonging to the debtor. The property was acquired by the debtor on 4 September 2000 and constituted developed land with a palace having a usable area of 997.2 m\textsuperscript{2} and two outbuildings. The buildings were not erected by the debtor. The property was not subject to any lease or tenancy agreement. The debtor likely carried out renovation works on the said property; however, its designated use did not change as a result. \\
